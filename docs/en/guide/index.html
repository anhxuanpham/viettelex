1. Enable the keyboard

iOS

Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard… → Vietnamese (VietTelex). While typing, tap 🌐 to switch to VietTelex. Try it right away in the app's Thử gõ box: vieejt → việt.

Android

Settings → System → Languages & input → On-screen keyboard → Manage → enable VietTelex, then select it (the app has a Chọn VietTelex button). The “may collect all the text you type” warning is Android's generic notice for every third-party keyboard — VietTelex runs entirely on-device.

Most settings take effect the next time the keyboard opens.

2. Telex / VNI options

The app's Kiểu Gõ tab. Telex and VNI are mutually exclusive.

  • VNI — tones as digits while inside a word: 1 acute, 2 grave, 3 hook, 4 tilde, 5 dot, 6 circumflex (â ê ô), 7 horn (ơ ư), 8 breve (ă), 9 đ, 0 remove: tie6ng1 vie6t5 → tiếng việt. Outside a word, digits stay digits. Picking VNI turns on the number row.
  • Simple Telex (Telex đơn giản) — a lone w stays w instead of ư.
  • Free tone placement (Bỏ dấu tự do) — tone keys can go anywhere in the word.
  • Quick Telex — doubled initial consonants: cc → ch, nn → ng, tt → th…
  • New-style tones (Bỏ dấu kiểu mới) — hoà, thuý instead of hòa, thúy.
  • Context-aware (Quyết định theo ngữ cảnh) — after an English word, an ambiguous next word stays English: “he is”, not “he í”.
  • Auto-restore English — non-Vietnamese words go back to what you typed (google, github…). Live spell check stops adding tones as soon as a word can't be Vietnamese.
  • Teencode spelling (off by default) — accepts wá, zui zẻ, kó, bíe, thík, gòy, ừk.

3. Fix tones after typing

Forgot a diacritic? No need to retype the word. Toggle: Tính Năng → Chính tả → Sửa dấu từ đã gõ (on by default). iOS Android macOS

  • Delete the space to reopen the word: tháy ␣ ⌫ a → thấy.
  • Put the cursor right after a word and type a tone key s f r x j: viêt + j → việt; chao + f → chào (VNI: digits 1–5, 0).

None of these has a button — try them now.

5. Swipe typing Experimental

Enable in Tính Năng → Thử nghiệm → Gõ vuốt. iPhone Android

  • Glide over the unaccented letters of a syllable and lift: v→i→e→t gives “việt”.
  • Type a tone key (s f r x j) right after to change the tone; ⌫ right after deletes the whole swiped word; the suggestion bar lists alternatives.
  • Swipe English words (on by default with swipe typing): check, mail, file, meeting… When a path is both Vietnamese and English (the/thế, can/cần), Vietnamese wins unless you're typing English, and the other option stays on the bar.
  • Neural swipe model (optional, off by default): adds the FUTO Swipe neural network, running fully on-device, to score alongside the built-in decoder — about 3 MB extra memory while the keyboard is open.
  • Disabled with VoiceOver/TalkBack and in password, email and URL fields. On iOS it's iPhone-only (not iPad).

6. Text shortcuts

Tính Năng → Gõ tắt. iOS Android macOS

  • Type the abbreviation, then space, Enter or punctuation: ko → không.
  • Case follows your typing (when the key is stored lowercase): ko → không, Ko → Không, KO → KHÔNG.
  • Keys with symbols or digits (->, k2) expand on space.
  • ⌫ right after an expansion restores what you typed.
  • No expansion right after a digit or / # @ (5h, /h3), or in password, email and URL fields.
  • The table starts empty; Thêm bộ gợi ý adds: ko, dc, đc, vs, mn, trc, cty, bsi, stk, -> (→).
  • Import/export YAML key: expansion — shared between Mac, iPhone and Android.

7. Suggestion bar & chips

VietTelex never rewrites your words — suggestions apply only when you tap them. Settings: Tính Năng → Gợi ý. The ⌄ button on the right collapses the bar.

  • Word suggestions that learn the words you use (stored on-device only). Personal dictionary (Từ điển cá nhân): view, search, delete learned words, add names and terms. Sensitive-word filter never proactively suggests profanity.
  • Mistap suggestions (Kiểu Gõ tab): nbjeeuf → nhiều, ohims → phím — shown on the bar, tap to replace.
  • Emoji suggestions matching the current word (yêu → ❤️).
  • Number chips: after typing a number — spell it out in words, format as money, quick maths (2+3 → 5, 12*3 → 36).
  • Paste button: right after you copy something, the bar shows a Paste card. iOS needs Full Access; to stop iOS asking every time: Settings → VietTelex → Paste from Other Apps → Allow.
  • “Thêm dấu” chip Plus (off by default): after a sentence typed without tones, one tap adds them all — hom nay troi dep → hôm nay trời đẹp.

8. Snippets & text tools

Toggle in Tính Năng → Gợi ý → Mẫu câu; manage in the Mẫu Câu tab. iOS Android

  • Tap ☰ on the suggestion bar for a grid of saved phrases; tap one to insert it. A short label (emoji) keeps the grid compact.
  • While the snippet grid is open, the 😊 key becomes a trash can that clears the whole field.
  • Live snippets: content starting with https:// is fetched when you tap it and the result is inserted (max 1000 bytes) — e.g. “🌐 IP” inserts your current IP address. iOS needs Full Access; without network the URL itself is inserted.
  • Import/export YAML - "👋 | Chào buổi sáng"; importing merges. Android can also add a snippet by sharing text from another app to VietTelex. iOS the ⚙️ tile at the end of the grid opens the Mẫu Câu tab.
  • Text tools Plus: the Aa chip at the top of the snippet grid — UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, remove tones; applied to the selection or the sentence before the cursor.

9. Clipboard & incognito

Tính Năng → Riêng tư. iOS Android

  • Clipboard history (off by default): the 📋 button on the bar opens up to 20 recent copies — tap to paste, pin to keep. Unpinned items expire after 1 hour (password/OTP-like: 2 minutes). Password fields and content marked sensitive are skipped. iOS needs Full Access.
  • Number-split chips Plus: when the copied text contains a bank account / phone number / OTP, a “Dán STK…” chip pastes just the number. Unlimited pins are also Plus.
  • Incognito mode: no word learning, no clipboard saving; a crossed-eye icon (iOS) / 🕶 (Android) shows on the keyboard. Android turns it on automatically in private-browser fields.

10. Emoji & kaomoji

  • The 😊 key opens a full emoji keyboard with a Recents 🕐 group.
  • Search emoji in Vietnamese: tap the search bar at the top; tones are optional — cuoi → 😀 “mặt cười”. Tap a result to insert it.
  • A kaomoji / special characters tab (^‿^) at the end of the category strip.

11. Appearance & one-handed

Tính Năng → Giao diện.

  • Themes & wallpaper: System, OLED dark and High contrast are always free; other themes (Hồng đào, Bạc hà, Trời xanh, Oải hương, Kính…) and a custom background image (overlay dimming, blur) are Plus. Images are compressed on-device.
  • Number row 1…0 above the letters; row height ±10.
  • Key preview pop-ups, haptics (iOS needs Full Access), faint Vᴛ logo on the space bar.
  • One-handed mode (phones): Off / Left / Right — shrinks the keyboard to one side; buttons in the empty strip switch sides or exit. Quick toggle via the gesture in section 4.
  • Smart key selection Experimental (on by default): a tap right on the edge between two keys picks the one that fits the word; a tap in the middle of a key always gives that key, and typed text is never auto-corrected.

12. Android only Android

  • Text-editing panel: tap the cursor icon on the suggestion bar — arrows, line start/end, select / select word / select all, copy / cut / paste, undo / redo; the last row holds the text tools Plus.
  • Telex for hardware keyboards (Tính Năng → Bàn phím cứng): type Telex/VNI on Bluetooth/USB keyboards, Samsung DeX, Chromebook; Ctrl/Alt shortcuts still reach the app.
  • Number / phone / date fields open a dedicated number pad.

13. Backup & sync

Giới Thiệu tab → Sao lưu & đồng bộ.

  • Export / import a backup file: settings, shortcuts, snippets (and learned words if “Kèm từ đã học” is on) — one file that opens on iPhone, iPad and Android. Import: settings follow the file, shortcuts and snippets are merged.
  • iCloud sync iOS Plus: settings, shortcuts and learned words follow you to other devices on the same Apple ID. macOS syncs Telex options and the shortcut table with iPhone/iPad.
  • Android also backs settings up to Google (system backup) when you enable it.

14. On macOS macOS

  • Fix tones after typing, text shortcuts (⌥↩ for a line break in an expansion), VNI, teencode — same as on mobile.
  • Text tools: select text in any app, then pick from the VietTelex menu — add tones to the selection (toi di hoc → tôi đi học), UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, remove tones. An add-tones hotkey works in any app (needs Accessibility).
  • Switch hotkey: press and release modifier keys alone to toggle VietTelex.
  • An app types wrong? See Typing modes in VietTelex (Vietnamese).

15. Full Access & Plus

  • iOS: the keyboard is fully usable without Full Access. It's only needed for haptics, live snippets (https://), the Paste button, clipboard history and saving learned words. VietTelex never collects or sends what you type.
  • Android: network access is used only for live snippets you create.
  • VietTelex Plus (one-time purchase): premium themes & wallpaper, whole-sentence tones, advanced clipboard, iCloud sync, text tools, a thank-you badge. All typing features are free forever; right now every Plus feature is unlocked for everyone.
